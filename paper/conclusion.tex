\section{Conclusion and Remaining Derivations}
The revised substrate budget yields the exact load $\mathcal L=1491/1376$ once the additive unit-weight rule is specified. Its coordinate term is the complement of the earlier payload fraction $171/172$. Under the rate and inverse-clustering rules, the draft inputs give
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
H_0^{\rm late}&=73.032994\ldots\ \mathrm{km/s/Mpc},\\
S_8^{\rm obs}&=0.767828303\ldots.
\end{split}
\end{equation}
For a fixed load, the propagated input uncertainties are approximately $0.542$ km/s/Mpc and $0.01200$, respectively. They do not include uncertainty in the constitutive rules.

The formalization supplies internal finite-budget consequences: a saturated register cannot reversibly absorb an independent input without extra outcomes or capacity, and independent equal-area cells have information proportional to area. Neither consequence by itself selects a spacetime metric, a radiation mechanism or the physical entropy coefficient.

A complete dual description still needs an evolution law connecting substrate states to expansion and growth observables. The proposed primordial tilt, zero tensor contribution, neutrino ordering and dark-sector interpretation are further physical hypotheses requiring their own derivations and data tests. The paper's numerical comparisons should be assessed jointly under a specified cosmological history rather than identified with resolutions of those tests by arithmetic alone.
