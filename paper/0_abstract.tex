\begin{abstract}
We investigate a finite-capacity substrate model of cosmological scaling. The revised load factor includes channel, boundary and reserved-coordinate terms,
$\mathcal L=1+1/(2\nu)+\chi/\Delta+1/(D\Delta)=1491/1376\approx1.0835756$.
With the draft early-rate input $67.4$ km/s/Mpc and the stated multiplier rule, this gives $H_0^{\rm late}\approx73.03$ km/s/Mpc. Applying the same factor inversely to the draft clustering input $0.832$ gives $S_8\approx0.76783$. These are conditional rescalings, not an absolute-rate derivation or a cosmological likelihood result. We link the reserved-coordinate term to the earlier payload budget and prove finite-capacity and independent-cell information results in Lean. A dynamical derivation of the cosmological response, the primordial spectrum and black-hole thermodynamic normalization remains necessary for a complete substrate account.
\end{abstract}
